# Will the Customer Accept the Coupon?

This project explores coupon acceptance behavior using a dataset of customers and five coupon types. I focused mainly on the Bar coupon and briefly explored the Coffee House coupon in the Independent Investigation section.

## Key Findings

### Bar Coupon:
- Drivers who go to bars more than once a month are more likely to accept bar coupons.
- Acceptance is higher among those under the age of 30.
- Drivers with passengers who are not kids and who do not work in "Farming, Fishing & Forestry" are more likely to accept the coupons.
- Lower income drivers were less likely to accept.

### Coffee House Coupon:
- People over the age of 40 were slightly less likely to accept compared to those under 40.

Using pandas and seaborn, I analyzed coupon acceptance patterns and visualized differences across customers. The goal was to uncover patterns that influence whether someone accepts a coupon offer or not.
